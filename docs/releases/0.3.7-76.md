# WAI Remote 0.3.7 — Mac 76 delivered, iPhone 73 in TestFlight

Mac 76 corrects input routing in save dialogs and native menus. Menu selection stays scoped to the clicked menu and preserves typing in its originating document. Disabled items perform no action. The remote pointer remains separate, and single-window grants retain background input.

The signed universal Mac 76 build was published to the normal Sparkle feed on 1 November 2026 at 15:09 UTC. The app and DMG passed Apple notarization, stapling and mounted-app checks; the published bytes and Sparkle signatures were verified. Source: `d5046e65a023608fefa969864abfbe9ce82ea6ff`. DMG SHA-256: `eee5e3342f939a83a79599469ee1f6a1bc5f80e58a29285186aaaf05445629b5`.

The MacBook completed Sparkle 75→76 with the expected executable hash and unchanged saved-computer/access state. The Mini then completed the normal 74→76 update, automatically reconnected, and showed build 76 in Settings. Build 75 remained a preview; it was not promoted to the normal feed.

[Mac 76 CI](https://github.com/mikwiseman/wai-remote/actions/runs/41318854226) passed 247 Swift tests with three existing skips, the gateway race tests and the web build. Native coverage includes compact save-panel Unicode, physical keys and Escape; expanded filename editing and Cancel; and menu selection, disabled/modified taps, overlay exclusions and continued typing. This does not establish that Escape dismisses every save-panel state.

Live Mini testing confirmed save-panel filename editing with Unicode and physical keys, and Cancel after the app was active. It also exposed a foreground gap: a desktop tap could raise a window while the previous app retained focus. The first tap on an inactive close-unsaved Cancel panel activated TextEdit without dismissing the panel; the next tap dismissed it. System Settings dropdowns did not open, including after Settings was made foreground through its Dock icon. These cases are not recorded as passing.

Build 77 addresses the reproduced background-host activation defect. Three-process tests distinguish the regular WAI host from the earlier accessory-app fixture and verify the clicked app actually becomes foreground. Mac 77 remains an unpromoted preview; a generic native picker passing is not proof for all System Settings dropdowns.

iPhone 0.3.7 (73) is available in the existing internal TestFlight group. App Store Connect readback at 14:21 UTC on 1 November confirmed `VALID / IN_BETA_TESTING`, preserving the existing group and two testers. Its [iOS CI](https://github.com/mikwiseman/wai-remote/actions/runs/41314088725/job/124870356973) passed all 45 tests. The build fixes keyboard-dismissal timing; four rendered SessionView tests cover tap coordinates, zoom/pan/Fit, hold-drag, typing and overlays. Physical iPhone installation, scrolling feel, cellular handover and end-to-end latency remain unverified.
