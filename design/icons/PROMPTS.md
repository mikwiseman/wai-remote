# Icon generation brief

Create one production-ready macOS app icon source: square, opaque, unmasked, flat front view, generous negative space. Keep the symbol around 62% of the canvas, with no text, letters, miniature interface, surrounding mockup, pre-rounded corners or drop shadow. The mark must also survive as a one-color menu bar template.

## 01-portal

An open square portal with a smaller solid rounded square passing through its upper-right corner. The outer portal is one thick continuous stroke with a deliberate opening in the upper-right, so the silhouette evokes both a screen and reaching another space. Rich dark evergreen mark on warm porcelain ivory.

## 02-ribbon

One broad vermilion ribbon folds once into a diagonal bridge between two short parallel ends. Extremely simple, geometric, distinctive; reads as a subtle letter R only on second look. Vermilion red with a single darker fold on warm white.

## 03-windows

Two offset rounded rectangular window outlines share one continuous diagonal join. Bold small-size silhouette, no screen stand, no arrow, no extra detail. Off-white mark on a deep forest-green full-bleed square background.

## 04-orbit

Two bold circular dots connected by one flowing bent line around a square negative-space window, like two computers finding each other. A confident asymmetric minimal symbol. Luminous pale lime mark on charcoal full-bleed background.

## 05-reach

A single thick right-angle corner creates an open frame and a solid diagonal cursor wedge reaches into it. Form a unified generous geometric glyph rather than a cursor pasted on a monitor. Cobalt blue mark on warm porcelain ivory.

## 06-fold

A minimal folded rectangular sheet opens into a portal: just two large simple intersecting planes, one ivory plane and one warm apricot plane, on a deep midnight-blue background. An intriguing architectural monogram, balanced flat front view, no perspective mockup.

## 07-near

Two large opposing rounded chevrons nearly meet, leaving a tiny square window in negative space at the center. Friendly bold soft geometry with precise corners, intentional memorable silhouette, no double-arrow button appearance. Off-white mark on a saturated coral-red full-bleed square background.
